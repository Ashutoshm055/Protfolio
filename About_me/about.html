Research Interest

Cloud Computing

My primary research passion lies in Cloud Computing, with a specific focus on Green Cloud Computing. I'm fascinated not just by the challenge of building scalable and resilient cloud infrastructure, but by the critical need to do so sustainably. My interest is in exploring novel approaches to minimize the energy consumption of data centers, optimize resource allocation for a lower carbon footprint, and design architectures that deliver high performance with greater environmental responsibility. I believe the future of technology depends on creating solutions that are both powerful and conscious of their ecological impact.

Data Analytics

My interest in Data Analytics is centered on its intersection with cloud platforms. I am less drawn to theoretical analysis and more excited by the practical application of data pipelines that can be implemented and scaled directly on the cloud. I enjoy working with cloud-native tools to process massive datasets, build real-time analytics systems, and make data-driven insights accessible and efficient. For me, the true potential of data analytics is unlocked when it's powered by the elasticity and robustness of cloud infrastructure.

Human-Computer Interaction

Ultimately, technology is built to serve people, which is why I am deeply interested in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). My focus is on how the backend power of cloud computing and data analytics can be translated into more intuitive, responsive, and meaningful user experiences. I want to explore how we can leverage data to create personalized interfaces and how robust cloud infrastructure can ensure that digital interactions are seamless and accessible, effectively bridging the gap between complex systems and the end-user.
